Correct the strict_* family's direction contract: 10 of 16 were looser, not stricter

The experts audit reported that `_strict_selector.thresholded_decision`'s docstring
promises "a stricter binary cutoff" and that 10 of the 16 `strict_*` variants use a
threshold BELOW the strategy they claim to tighten. Measured, confirmed, and fixed.

WHY NOTHING CAUGHT IT

  * only 5 of 16 modules declared BASE_STRATEGY_ID, and those 5 were imported by
    nothing, so the only machine-readable statement of the link was inert;
  * THESIS is read by no code (re-verified: no non-comment reference to it exists
    in src/ or scripts/), so a false sentence in it could not fail anything;
  * no test pinned any strict_* threshold -- `grep THRESHOLD tests/` returns
    nothing for the whole family.

THE MEASUREMENT

Resolving each base by function identity (the module imports it as `_base_predict`)
rather than by parsing the import gives all 16 pairs, where a regex gave 5 and then
a naive annotated-declaration regex gave 7. Direction census: 10 LOOSER, 6 tighter,
0 equal. And every one of the 10 looser variants emits MORE signals than its base,
without exception, and 9 of the 10 have LOWER precision:

  strict_oversold_rebound_v2    T=0.4496 vs base 0.8200   102.43x signals  -9.01pp
  strict_turnover_weak_to_strong_v2 T=0.7027 vs 0.9000     20.24x signals  -1.12pp
  strict_platform_breakout_v2   T=0.6075 vs 0.8000          9.54x signals  -1.56pp
  strict_platform_breakout      T=0.6500 vs 0.8000          6.97x signals  -1.45pp
  strict_leader_momentum_v2     T=0.7885 vs 0.9000          4.52x signals  -3.14pp
  strict_washout_complete_v2    T=0.7037 vs 0.8200          2.68x signals  -0.81pp
  strict_washout_complete       T=0.7200 vs 0.8200          2.45x signals  -0.66pp
  strict_first_board_breakout_v2 T=0.7000 vs 0.9000         2.41x signals  +0.48pp
  strict_gap_follow_through_v2  T=0.6909 vs 0.7200          1.45x signals  -1.15pp
  strict_accumulation_base_v2   T=0.4621 vs 0.9000          base emits 0 signals

strict_oversold_rebound_v2 is the clearest case: its THESIS said "A higher cutoff
keeps only oversold rebounds with the clearest exhaustion", and it emits 31,754
signals at 2.93% precision -- below the 3.035% scanned-grid base rate, i.e. worse
than picking at random.

THE FIX

  * BASE_STRATEGY_ID declared in all 16 modules (and exported in the 11 that have
    an __all__; the 5 others never had one, checked against git).
  * Single-line THESIS claims rewritten on the 7 that said "A higher cutoff keeps
    only ...", and the parenthesised multi-line ones on the 3 the first pass could
    not match, plus 9 "high-precision" docstrings that measured LOWER precision.
  * THRESHOLD VALUES ARE NOT TOUCHED. They are fitted outputs of the 100-card
    development scan; moving a fitted number to match a sentence written about it
    would be backwards. The sentence was what was wrong.
  * The change is text-only: THESIS, docstrings and __all__ are read by no logic,
    and all 16 modules still import, the registry still reports 36 strategies with
    16 strict_*, and the test suite passes.

THE GUARD, AND THE HOLE THE TEST FOUND IN IT

The audit now requires every strict_* variant to declare an existing, exported base,
to have a readable THRESHOLD for itself AND its base, and not to claim a direction
its threshold contradicts. Two fail-opens were found in this new guard by its own
negative test (scripts/scratch/_probe_strict_guard.py, now 6/6 caught):

  1. the threshold regex `[:=][^=]*?` cannot cross the `=` of an annotated
     `THRESHOLD: float = 0.90`, so it resolved 7 of 16 pairs and reported
     `looser=1` instead of 10 -- the same fail-open the guard exists to prevent,
     reproduced inside the guard. An `unparsed` list now turns any such skip into a
     FAILURE, and a coverage check requires the census to sum to the family size.
  2. the prose test built one module-wide blob and skipped the module if "superset"
     appeared anywhere in it, so restoring `"""Development-only high-precision
     leader-momentum selector."""` passed. It is now evaluated per line, and a line
     carrying a forbidden claim must itself carry the honest qualifier.

560 checks, 0 problems. 10 tests pass.
